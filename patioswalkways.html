Wilson - Stone Work Built to Last

What type of design can you suggest?

There are a large variety of designs you can create depending on the type of brick or stone you choose. Brick can be installed in the popular basket weave pattern, which is flexible and turns corners well. You could also choose a herringbone pattern or a sunburst layout with brick radiating from a center point. Brick can be combined with river stone or used in a border around a concrete patio.

Every different terrain lends itself to a unique design, and adding a stone walkway, Pavestone patio or retaining wall can add to the beauty of the site if done properly. The color and shape of stone chosen should fit nicely with the overall landscape. Wilson Landscape design will work with you to create a beautiful and functional design. To get ideas, pickup a copy of a home and garden magazine or view different types of stone available at the Pavestone Website.

Why choose Wilson for stone work?

We know the secrets of designing a quality stonework, stone patio installation and building solid stone retaining walls. They lie in proper planning, consideration of the site composition, and most importantly in laying each stone firmly in place.

Don't pay for work that falls apart months later. We put forth our best effort every time to make sure we only have to do a job once.
