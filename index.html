Wilson Landscape

Wilson Landscape is a full service commercial and residential company for outdoor construction and landscape installation, including patios, stonework, drainage, irrigation, planting, and lawn care. We have been serving the DFW Metroplex since 1987. We pride ourselves in providing our customers with excellent workmanship, superior service and quality materials that meet the highest of horticultural standards.

WHAT WE BUILD AND INSTALL

Patios, flagstone, and retaining walls lead the work, with drainage, irrigation, planting, and lawn care to finish the property.

HOW A PROJECT GETS BUILT

01 | Site visit

We walk the property with you and look at the patio, stone, drainage, or irrigation work you need.

02 | Project plan

We map the construction, materials, and installation so the scope is clear before work starts.

03 | Construction and installation

We build patios, walls, drains, and sprinkler systems, then install planting where it belongs.

04 | Planting and lawn care

We finish with trees, shrubs, and turf, then keep the property cared for with mowing and maintenance.
