Lawn Mowing

Lawn Mowing & Weekly Yard Maintenance Services

We offer weekly lawn mowing service, professional lawn care, yard maintenance services, flowerbed installation, and lawn maintenance programs for a wide range of properties—from residential yards in Flower Mound and Lewisville to fully landscaped, 100-acre commercial sites in the DFW area. By mowing, pruning, and weeding flowerbeds, we help maintain the beauty of your home or office.

Serving Lewisville, Flower Mound, and DFW Areas

Call us today for a free, no-obligation estimate on Weekly Lawn Mowing or Full Service Yard Maintenance in Lewisville and Flower Mound. Be sure to ask about our discounted annual lawn care programs.

Weekly Lawn Mowing Service

This service includes weekly lawn mowing and edging. We bill per mow at the end of each month.

  • Mowing and Edging
  • Irrigation checks performed monthly (repairs and parts billed separately)
  • Weed-eating, Bed weeding - Additional Charge
  • Ground cover clean-up and debris removal - Additional Charge
  • Fertilize grass - Additional Charge
  • Fertilize ground cover - Additional Charge
  • Shrub trimming and shaping - Additional Charge
  • Tree pruning and thinning - Additional Charge

Full Service Lawn Care

Full Service maintenance is billed at a flat rate at the end of each month.

Features include:

  • Mowing, weed-eating, bed weeding, ground cover clean-up and debris removal
  • Fertilize grass
  • Fertilize ground cover
  • Irrigation checks performed monthly (repairs and parts billed separately)
  • Shrub trimming and shaping
  • Tree pruning and thinning - Additional Charge

All full service plans also include seasonal maintenance as follows:

  • Perennial clean up, cut and trim down flowers after the first hard freeze
  • Pre-emergent weed control (Spring/Fall)
  • Annual Crape Myrtle trimming (Optional) - Additional Charge
  • Scalp and overseed with Winter Rye - Additional Charge
