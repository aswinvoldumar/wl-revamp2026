French Drain Installation

French Drain Installation & Drainage Solutions

Wilson Landscape provides expert French drain installation and complete yard drainage solutions for residential and commercial properties across Lewisville, Flower Mound, and the greater Dallas Metroplex.

Located conveniently in the Lewisville–Flower Mound area, we specialize in designing and installing custom drainage systems that effectively redirect surface and subsurface water—preventing flooding, erosion, and foundation damage while protecting your landscape investment.

Our French Drain Installation Includes:

  • Comprehensive site inspection and water flow analysis
  • Custom drainage design with precise slope and elevation planning
  • Gravel-lined trench construction for efficient water movement
  • High-quality perforated piping and geotextile filtration fabric
  • Backfill and surface restoration that blends seamlessly with your landscape
  • Protection for lawns, garden beds, driveways, and home foundations
  • Optional sump pump integration for enhanced water redirection
  • One-year warranty on workmanship and materials
  • Maintenance plans for long-term system performance

We use professional-grade materials and proven techniques to ensure every French drain performs efficiently through heavy rains and seasonal changes. Our goal is to keep your property dry, stable, and looking great all year long.

Our Drainage Services Include:

  • French Drain Design and Installation
  • Surface and Subsurface Drainage Systems
  • Catch Basins and Channel Drain Installation
  • Grading and Slope Correction
  • Retaining Wall Drainage Systems
  • Foundation Moisture Management
  • Sump Pump Installation and Maintenance

Understanding Your Drainage System

Our team takes the time to explain how your French drain system works—so you can maintain proper drainage and prevent future issues. We design every system based on your property’s specific grading and soil conditions, ensuring maximum efficiency and minimal maintenance.

With Wilson Landscape, you gain peace of mind knowing your home and landscape are protected from excess water year-round. Our experience, precision, and commitment to quality make us the preferred choice for drainage and French drain installation in North Texas.

Professional French Drain Installation Services

  • Expert drainage design tailored to your property layout
  • Licensed installers with extensive French drain experience
  • High-grade gravel and perforated piping for lasting performance
  • Clean and efficient installation with minimal lawn disruption
  • Comprehensive solutions for surface and subsurface drainage issues
  • Locally based in Flower Mound and serving all nearby communities
