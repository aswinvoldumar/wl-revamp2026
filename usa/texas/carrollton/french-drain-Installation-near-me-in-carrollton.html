Why Choose a French Drain?

A properly installed French drain is one of the most effective and long-lasting solutions for managing surface and subsurface water. Our Carrollton French drain systems are built to:

  • Eliminate standing water and soggy areas in your yard
  • Protect your home’s foundation and landscaping from water damage
  • Promote healthy soil drainage and lawn growth
  • Integrate seamlessly into your existing landscape design
Residential & Commercial Drainage Solutions

Wilson Landscape provides reliable French drain installation for both residential and commercial properties in Carrollton. From small backyard drainage systems to complex commercial site solutions, our experienced team delivers durable, efficient installations that withstand heavy rain and long-term exposure. We also offer grading, retaining walls, and full landscape restoration to ensure complete water management and visual harmony.

Our Installation Process

Every French drain installation begins with a detailed on-site assessment to pinpoint problem areas, water flow patterns, and soil type. We then design and construct a gravel-lined trench with perforated piping that safely directs water away from your property. Our precise and careful installation process ensures maximum performance and minimal disruption to your landscape.

Keep Your Yard Dry and Damage-Free with Professional French Drain Installation

Why Your Property Needs a French Drain System

  • Prevents standing water and flooding in your yard after heavy rain
  • Protects your home’s foundation and basement from water damage
  • Improves soil stability and prevents erosion around your property
  • Directs excess groundwater away, keeping lawns and gardens healthy
  • Increases property value by maintaining a dry, usable outdoor space

Key Features

  • Effective water redirection to prevent yard flooding
  • High-quality perforated pipes and gravel for lasting drainage
  • Protects your foundation and basement from water damage
  • Reduces soil erosion and maintains lawn stability
  • Discreet installation that blends seamlessly with your landscape
  • Low-maintenance system designed for long-term performance

Material Options

  • High-quality perforated PVC or corrugated pipes for superior water flow
  • Clean, angular gravel or crushed stone for optimal drainage efficiency
  • Heavy-duty landscape fabric to prevent soil clogging and extend system life
  • Durable catch basins and end caps for precise water collection and redirection
  • Eco-friendly materials designed for long-term, low-maintenance performance

Design & Customization

  • System layouts customized to suit your lawn and garden needs
  • Smart irrigation options: drip, spray, and rotor systems
  • Optional add-ons like rain sensors, timers, and zone controls
  • Work with our experts to design an efficient watering solution

Maintenance Tips

  • Regularly check sprinkler heads for clogs or damage
  • Adjust spray patterns to prevent overwatering or runoff
  • Inspect pipes and valves for leaks each season
  • Test and update the controller settings for optimal efficiency
