Irrigation Systems

Sprinkler Installation Sprinkler Repair & Irrigation Services

Wilson offers a wide range of sprinkler installation, sprinkler repair and irrigation services for Residential and Commercial properties in Lewisville, Flower Mound and throughout the Dallas Metroplex.

Conveniently located in the Lewisville - Flower Mound area, we use the latest technology available to allow for proper sprinkler coverage and maximum water conservation.

New installations include the following:

  • City registration as irrigation contractor for the project
  • Irrigation system design to scale with all hydraulic pressure calculations
  • Wireless rain freeze gauge
  • Backflow assembly testing
  • All city permits, city inspections and required meetings with city inspectors
  • Warranty 1 year parts and labor on pipe, sprinkler heads and fittings
  • Warranty 3 years on electrical components and timers
  • All designs done with 100 coverage overlap with minimal overspray to conserve water
  • Optional wifi sprinkler control module to allow timer control from computer, tablet or cell phone
  • Flat hourly rates with no service call and 24 hour service in most cases
  • Broken pipe and head repairs
  • Valve replacement or repair
  • Solenoid replacement or repair
  • Wire locates service for broken wire or loose wire fittings
  • Valve locates for missing and non-working valves as well as valves that have been buried or have grass grown over and are not visible
  • Foundation drip line conversions or additions. Drip zones around foundations are extremely helpful in maintaining proper moisture around our homes here in north Texas
  • Pool reconnects after pool installation **Please note it will save time and money for us to cut and cap mainline prior to pool construction. In most cases we can disconnect pool construction zones and leave remainder of sprinkler zones working during the pool construction process.
  • Retrofits due to add-ons or construction

Year round wireless rain freeze gauge installations $179.95 to prevent icing and wasting water during rainy season.
We service and install all major brands including Rainbird, Toro, Weathermatic, Hunter and Irritrol.

Our irrigation services include:

  • Irrigation Consultation, Design and Installation
  • Sprinkler System Installation
  • Sprinkler System Repair and Troubleshooting
  • Landscape Drainage (residential to large commercial)
  • French Drains and Sump Pumps
  • State Backflow Inspection and Certification (state required)

Understanding Your Irrigation System

We will install the most water-efficient design around, but the amount of water applied to the landscape is ultimately decided by you.

Wilson helps you to understand and operate your irrigation system in a water-efficient manner, saving money for you and protecting resources for generations to come. As an educated consumer, you will understand the basics of plant selection and placement, as well as how much water is actually needed in the landscape.
